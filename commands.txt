Adding Transactions
-------------------

English:
  "Add expense 5000 food"          — Add 5,000 expense in Food category
  "Add income 50000 salary"        — Add 50,000 income in Salary category
  "Log expense 5k lunch"           — Add 5,000 expense with note "lunch"
  "Expense 3000 transport today"   — Add 3,000 transport expense for today
  "Add expense 2000 groceries yesterday"  — Add 2,000 groceries for yesterday

  Supported amount formats:
    5000, 5,000, 5k, 1.5k, 2m

  Category keywords (English):
    Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Health, Fun, Education, Other
    Salary, Freelance, Bonus, Gift

  Income keywords:
    income, salary, earned, earning, received, bonus, gift, freelance

  Expense keywords:
    expense, spent, paying, paid, bought, purchase, food, lunch, etc.

  Date modifiers:
    today, yesterday, tomorrow

Burmese:
  "ထွက်ငွေ ၅၀၀၀ အစာ"             — Add 5,000 expense in Food category
  "ငွေဝင် ၅၀၀၀၀ လစာ"             — Add 50,000 income in Salary category
  "ထွက်ငွေ ၅k ကော်ဖီ"             — Add 5,000 expense with note "coffee"
  "ထွက်ငွေ ၃၀၀၀ သယ်ယူပို့ဆောင်ရေး ဒီနေ့" — Add transport expense for today

  Supported amount formats:
    ၅၀၀၀, ၅,၀၀၀, ၅k, ၁.၅k, ၂m
    (Myanmar digits: ၀ ၁ ၂ ၃ ၄ ၅ ၆ ၇ ၈ ၉)

  Category keywords (Burmese):
    အစာ (Food), ကား (Transport), ဈေးဝယ် (Shopping),
    ဘီလ် (Bills), ဆေး (Health), ဂိမ်း (Fun),
    ကျောင်း (Education), လစာ (Salary),
    အလွတ်တန်းအလုပ် (Freelance), ဆု (Bonus), ခုံ (Gift)

  Income keywords (Burmese):
    ဝင်ငွေ, လစာ, မေးငွေ, အထူ်, ပေးငွေ, လက်ခဏာ

  Expense keywords (Burmese):
    ထွက်ငွေ, အသုံ်, အကုန်, ဝယ်, ရရှိ, လက်ခဏာ

  Date modifiers (Burmese):
    ဒီနေ့ (today), မနေ့က (yesterday)

Spending Queries
----------------

  English: "spent", "spending", "pay", "paid", "bought", "purchase", "expense"
  Burmese: ထွက်ငွေ, အသု်သုံး, အကုန်, ဝယ်, ရရှိ, ပေးငွေ

Category Queries
----------------

  English: "spent on food", "food spending this month", "transport this week"
  Burmese: "အစာ အသု်သုံးမယ်", "ဒီလမျိုးဆက်သုံးထားတဲ့်"

Tips:
  - You can mix English and Burmese phrases
  - Numbers can be written in Myanmar digits (၀-၉) or ASCII digits (0-9)
  - The bot remembers your last used language setting
  - Say "help" anytime to see this information again
